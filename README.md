# 腎囊醫訣 / Shen nang yi jue

《腎囊醫訣》（Shen nang yi jue）是1874年由寄傲軒印行的中文外科譯著，牛津博德利館目記出版地為[Amoy]。原著者為Thomas Blizard Curling，Patrick Manson譯介並增補解剖及其自身觀察，英文序署Amoy（廈門）。內容涉及外腎、腎囊與腎系諸症、病例、歷史外科技術及圖說，前置文字另有中文序、英文序、總目與中英詞表，可供晚清醫學翻譯、術語及醫學圖像史研究。本版據牛津博德利圖書館Sinica 1845三冊掃描，提供497個原印文字、圖說及圖中文字面的JSON、TXT與TEI，保留裝訂冊原序及印本卷號衝突。本版經AI讀稿比對與原圖覆核，疑讀仍標記，尚未經人類全文校勘；方法與限制見METHODS.md。

Shen nang yi jue (1874), published by Ji ao xuan at [Amoy] according to the Bodleian catalogue, is Patrick Manson’s Chinese translation and adaptation of a work by Thomas Blizard Curling. Its English preface, signed at Amoy, describes additional anatomical material and observations by Manson. The book discusses historical conditions of the testis, scrotum and spermatic cord, case records, surgery and illustrated explanations, with Chinese and English prefaces, a contents list and a bilingual vocabulary. It can support research on late Qing medical translation, terminology and medical illustration. This version provides JSON, TXT and TEI for 497 original printed text, caption and labelled illustration faces from the three bound volumes of Bodleian Sinica 1845, retaining their physical order and conflicting printed volume numbers. This version follows comparison of AI readings and checks against the source images; uncertain readings remain marked, full human collation is pending, and methods and limitations are described in METHODS.md.
