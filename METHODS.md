# 方法與AI角色 / Methods

The source was read from fixed 1000px previews and their lossless 500px-wide half-face PNG splits, with full-frame context and selected centrally acquired original-region crops for local uncertainties. Actual preview heights vary and are recorded per source. Diagram text may extend across a geometric half and remains attached to its semantic source face. Four disjoint initial-reading parts were integrated by stable face and unit IDs without rewriting their text. Local rereads by the same initial reader are recorded separately from the original raw versions; they are not independent collation. AI source-reading and text-encoding assistance used an OpenAI native subscription service; the actual model identity, effort, tokens and cash are not exposed by the available execution evidence and remain null, rather than inferred from requested settings. No independent reading manuscript was used in this integration. Original selected codepoints, source punctuation, historical terminology, apparent errors and numerical inconsistencies are retained. Small notes, source marks, marginal writing of unknown responsibility, captions, diagram labels and imprints remain separate. Engineering checks cover exact text and ordered-ID roundtrips, source metadata, official TEI Relax NG and CFF schemas; they do not measure accuracy. Schematron, full scholarly apparatus, full human collation, expert review and a measured error rate are not provided. The private source images, complete working records, prompts and specialized production software are not public, so a complete image-to-text rerun is not supplied. This is a historical text resource, not modern clinical guidance.

實際模型、effort、input/output/thinking/cache tokens及cash未由可得執行紀錄揭露，均為null；不從請求型號或訂閱名稱推定。完整提示、原始影像、工作紀錄及專用製作程式維持私有；本包提供公開資料的來源連結與格式回讀所需結構，不承諾完整影像到文字流程可重跑。整合只讀四份已固定初稿，沒有接觸獨立讀字稿。
